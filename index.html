About the Film

The burden of power — and the choice of what to do with it.

Chain of Power is a Jamaican supernatural action drama. After inheriting a mysterious necklace her father died protecting, Amanda becomes the target of ruthless enemies who will stop at nothing to possess its unimaginable abilities.

As long-buried secrets about her family's past come to light, she must uncover the legacy she was born into — before the necklace, and the power it holds, falls into the wrong hands.

As secrets unravel and loyalties crumble, Amanda must decide whether to embrace the necklace's power or destroy it before it destroys everyone around her.

Some people fight for money. Some fight for revenge. They fight for power.
